Use the shared `sm` height for menu, listbox, select, combobox, command menu and navigation rows. Select and Listbox rows drop from 40px to 36px at comfortable density. These rows measure 32px at compact density and 40px at touch density.

Use 16px text for medium fields to match medium buttons. Fields use at least 16px text at touch density and on touch devices at any density, to avoid focus zoom on iOS.
