## Space

Spacing uses a 4px grid with 2px half steps: `2 · 4 · 6 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32`. Every gap comes from this list, written as a token. CodeImage currently writes the same 8px gap as `'8px'` in some files and `spacing['2']` in others. The kit is where those two spellings become one.

### Proximity does the grouping

There are no lines between groups, so space alone shows what belongs together. The rule is simple: the space inside a group is always smaller than the space between groups. A title and its description sit 2–4px apart. Rows in a group touch. Groups sit 16–24px apart. A heading has more space above it than below, so it attaches to the content that follows.

### The shell

Everything sits 8px from the window edge and 8px from its neighbours. The header has no bar of its own. It is as tall as its controls (36px) and reads as the first row of islands, with its edges aligned to the islands below.

## Radius follows nesting

A corner inside another corner should be concentric with it: **inner radius = outer radius − the padding between them**. If both use the same radius, the gap between them is thick at the corners and thin along the sides, and the inner box looks crooked even though it is not.

The radius therefore depends on where an element sits, not on what kind of component it is:

| Level | Radius |
| --- | --- |
| Island, floating bar, command menu | 22px |
| Group, popover, dialog, nudge | 16px |
| Field inside a 22px surface | 14px |
| Control: button, segmented track, option card | 12px |
| Row: menu row, nav item, segment | 10px |
| Badge, tag, keycap | 6px |
| Dot, switch, pill | full |

The kit currently has eleven radius tokens, and some differ by only 1–2px (7, 9, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18…). CodeImage uses 22, 18 and 16px for things that are all "a floating island". The table above is the target. The 7, 9 and 18px tokens go away.

Content near a large radius also needs room. CodeImage's logo moved to 16px from the island edge because at 8px it looked "troppo attaccato a sx", pushed too far into the corner.

## Elevation

An island in the layout has **no shadow**, because it is carved into the page and not placed on top of it. Only surfaces that float over something else get a shadow: menus, popovers, the command menu and dialogs. In dark mode they also get a 1px inner ring, since a shadow on a near-black page is barely visible.

A floating surface has no backdrop unless it is modal. A picker that dimmed the page would hide the content it is supposed to change.

## Size

Controls are **36px** tall on desktop and **40–44px** on a phone. A tighter build, with 28px rows and a 300px panel, was the first version of CodeImage's new editor and was rejected as too compact.

- Hit targets are at least 28px on desktop and 40px on a phone. An icon can be 16px, but its button cannot. A tiny mark, such as a line marker in the gutter, keeps a 24px invisible hit box.
- A segmented control has a fixed height in every container. In CodeImage one once grew to 200px inside a grid popover, because its height came from the parent.
- A row has a fixed 20px slot for its leading icon, so the text of every row lines up even when some rows have no icon.
- An icon-only button always has a tooltip and an accessible name.
